## The instrument is not the observation

The probe gives us a number. The field gives that number a place: shade depth,
root density, yesterday’s wind, and the hard edge where the service road still
holds heat.

## A useful difference

The useful change is not the largest value. It is the **difference that
survives a return visit**. GFM tables keep the ledger compact; the chart above
is an ordinary Haxe Client Component registered through Next’s required

### Protocol for the next pass

- Repeat within thirty minutes of the original observation.
- Record canopy cover before reading the probe.
- Photograph the litter layer, not only the instrument.
- Escalate a change only after the second return.

> A field note earns its place when another person can return and recover the
> same question.
